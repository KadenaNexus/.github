# Kadena Nexus

Community-built tools and sites for the Kadena blockchain, maintained since January 2026 by a small team after Kadena LLC wound down. Everything here runs on Kadena mainnet today or is being built for it.

**Website:** [kadena.ws](https://kadena.ws) · **Telegram:** [t.me/KadenaNexus](https://t.me/KadenaNexus) · **X:** [@KadenaSAUDI](https://x.com/KadenaSAUDI)

## Live

| Product | What it does | Code |
|---|---|---|
| [P2P Marketplace](https://marketplace.kadena.ws) | Trade KDA person to person, with the seller's coins held by a Pact escrow contract until the payment is confirmed | [marketplace](https://github.com/KadenaNexus/marketplace) |
| [Network Monitor](https://nodes.kadena.ws) | Live view of all 20 Chainweb chains: throughput, block time, first confirmation, node map | [network-monitor](https://github.com/KadenaNexus/network-monitor) |
| [Kadena Earn](https://earn.kadena.ws) | Funded community tasks paid in KDA through escrow | [earn](https://github.com/KadenaNexus/earn) |
| [Adoption Index](https://adoption.kadena.ws) | One daily score of whether Kadena Community Edition is being adopted | [adoption-index](https://github.com/KadenaNexus/adoption-index) |
| [Quantum-Safe Kadena](https://quantum.kadena.ws) | How Kadena holds up against quantum computers, and what a migration would take | |
| [Kadena Devs](https://kadenadevs.kadena.ws) | The projects and the team behind them | [devs-site](https://github.com/KadenaNexus/devs-site) |

## In development

Kadena Garden (browser mining), ChainProof (proof of existence), Kadena AgentPay AI (agent payments through Pact), KadenaTrace (follow KDA across chains) and Kadena Privacy. Each opens here when it launches.
